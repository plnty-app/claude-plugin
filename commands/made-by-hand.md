Run Plnty's creative skill Made by hand for the person, following the `creative-skills` skill, with `skill` set to "Made by hand".

Made by hand makes a short tabletop film: one thing shown being made, built, packed or prepared step by step on a table or another fixed surface, where the only people are hands (a product unboxed and set up, a bouquet tied, an order packed, a drink built, a gift wrapped), or a few small characters' journey told the same way. Two to six shots, each one step in real time, the same hero and the same hands in every one. Its first part writes the shots and makes their parts; drafting every shot is offered after it, at its own price.

It starts from the brief in words: one line about the film is enough, and Rothko writes the shots. For more control, the person gives six lines: the film (what it is for), the hero (its name, what it is, its pack), the hands (one person or a crew, gloved or bare, their sleeves), the places (each surface and where the camera looks from), the feel (pace, light, colours, shape, sound) and the steps (each shot: its place, its seconds, what it opens on, every step in order, what it ends on). Pictures of the hero are optional: any that come with the brief are the hero itself, kept exactly.
