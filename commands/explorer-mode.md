Run Plnty's creative skill Explorer mode for the person, following the `creative-skills` skill, with `skill` set to "Explorer mode".

Explorer mode takes a moodboard: several pictures of things, places and looks (a product or a character, a setting or a surface, a material, a style or a light). It needs at least two, and does best with four to twelve. It makes finished pictures from new mixes of them, not one edit.

The person's brief: $ARGUMENTS

If the brief says nothing about what to make of the pictures, ask who or what it is about, where, and the story or mood before anything else. If no pictures came with it, ask for them.
